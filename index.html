UAE Entry & Passport Rules

Passports must have a minimum validity of 6 months. Tourists overstaying their permitted duration incur standard ICP fines of AED 50/day. Ensure mandatory travel medical coverage.

VFS & Embassy Submissions

Biometric registration requires physical presence at VFS centers. Submit verifiable 6-month bank statements with valid bank seals to avoid delays.

IATA Ticketing & Flight Terms

Passenger names on tickets must match passport spelling exactly. Re-issuance fees and fare differences apply as per individual airline tariff rules.

Customs & Foreign Currency

Carrying cash above AED 60,000 (or equivalent) requires declaration upon arrival/departure. Prohibited goods, unprescribed medicines, and illegal items are strictly regulated.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Schengen VFS visa take to process?

Standard Schengen visa processing takes 10 to 14 working days from your VFS appointment date. Peak holiday seasons may take up to 21 working days.

What documents are needed for UAE Tourist Visas?

Passport front page copy (minimum 6 months validity), clear passport photo with white background, and return flight itinerary.

Are prices customizable for international tour packages?

Yes! All tour packages can be upgraded to 5-star hotels, private chauffeur transfers, or extended stays upon request with our desk agent.
